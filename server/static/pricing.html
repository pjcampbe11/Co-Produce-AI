Questions

What's a credit?

One credit ≈ one generation. A beat or tag costs 1, a flip 2, a remix 3, a full song 5. Failed jobs are automatically refunded.

Does it train on my own sound?

Yes — that's the point. Feed it your beats, lyrics, and sample library and it co-produces in your style. Your data and private models stay yours.

Can I self-host?

Absolutely. The whole stack is open on GitHub — run the dashboard locally or deploy the API + workers yourself. Plans are for the hosted service.

Cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed through Stripe; cancel whenever and you keep access through the period you've paid for.
